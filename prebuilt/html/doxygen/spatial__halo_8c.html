Detailed Description

The spatial halo: the parts of body B near this rank's share of body A, delivered as shadow objects. Design rationale: mftl_communication.h §6.

Author
Christian Gorges
Date
2026-08-16

Shape of one extension:

  1. one record per held body (id, extent, radius, envelope)
  2. publish the records to the graph neighbours count allgather + Iallgatherv
  3. per-triangle extents of every sendable body overlapped with 1
  4. match partners (grid or request list), classify and pack triangles
  5. exchange vertices and triangles one MFTLExchangeStart, 2 payloads
  6. rebuild one shadow object per arriving body MFTLBuildTopologyFromIndexList
  7. derive the candidate pairs (near mode); prune the directory

Work is organised per BODY, not per pair, so a triangle wanted by two partners on the same destination is packed – and reported – once. Two allocations per call are [nproc] send-buffer descriptor arrays (only neighbour entries are ever filled).

The owned extent of obj as a centre and half-width per axis.

Measured in two frames – raw and displaced by half a domain – keeping the tighter per axis: a share straddling the seam is contiguous in the shifted frame, one straddling the middle in the raw frame, and none can straddle both while its extent is under L/2 (the minimum-image assumption). The centre of the shifted frame is mapped back through the shift, which a (lo, hi) pair could not survive.

◆ MFTLSpatialHaloResolveRadii()

int MFTLSpatialHaloResolveRadii ( MFTL_Ensemble * ens,
MFTL_SpatialHalo * halo )

Resolves global_radius – half the LONGEST SIDE of the body's bounding box over all its holders ( \(= R\) for a sphere) – for every body this rank holds, and for the bodies its graph neighbours hold.

The GLOBAL box, so that envelopes scaled by it do not depend on the process count. Each holder contributes the box of its OWNED triangles in two frames, raw and shifted by half a domain, and the tighter extent per axis wins: a body straddling a periodic seam is contiguous in the shifted frame and would otherwise measure as domain-wide. Twelve MIN slots per body go through MFTLEnsembleReduceBodies(). The result is then published one hop along comm_graph, because a rank deciding what to SEND needs the size of the body it sends TOWARDS, which it may hold nothing of.

Parameters
ensThe ensemble.
haloThe halo whose radii are filled in.
Returns
MFTL_SUCCESS, or MFTL_ERROR on a NULL argument.
Collective:
Must be called by every rank of the ensemble's communicator. Ranks holding no bodies must call it too.

Turns the arrivals for one body into a valid, self-contained patch object (vertices first, then MFTLBuildTopologyFromIndexList()), and only then marks nothing owned.

  • Vertex rotation. The wire carries the TARGET of half-edge 3t+k; the index list wants the ORIGIN, origin(3t+k) = target(3t+(k+2)%3).
  • Boundary flags are restored, not derived. The topology build marks every twin-less half-edge as mesh boundary, but a patch's rim is a cut through a larger surface, so the transmitted flags overwrite the derived ones.

◆ MFTLExtendSpatialHalo()

int MFTLExtendSpatialHalo ( MFTL_Ensemble * ens,
const MFTL_SpatialHaloRequest * requests,
int n_requests,
MFTL_SpatialHalo * halo )

Exchanges the explicitly requested patches and rebuilds the affected shadow objects.

  1. Each rank publishes one record per body it HOLDS – (body id, centre, half-width, global radius, envelope), nine doubles – to its graph neighbours (count allgather + non-blocking allgatherv, overlapped with computing per-triangle extents). Payload O(local bodies x degree).
  2. For each request whose B body it holds, a rank sends every owned triangle of B (and its vertices) whose extent, widened by the margin, is near a neighbour's published extent of A. Per destination each triangle is packed once, however many partners want it.
  3. One MFTLExchangeStart() with two payloads; one patch per arriving body is rebuilt with MFTLBuildTopologyFromIndexList(), and the transmitted boundary flags overwrite the derived ones (a patch's cut rim is not a mesh boundary).

Extents are a centre plus a half-width, compared by minimum-image distance between centres; a (lo, hi) box would miss contacts across a periodic seam and, for any share containing one seam-straddling triangle, span the whole domain and ship everything (measured at np = 27: 314 triangles with boxes against 56). A rank's own extent is measured in the raw and the half-domain-shifted frame and the tighter kept. The per-triangle test is widened by the margin and so is the body-level pre-filter, which keeps the pre-filter strictly more permissive than the test it short-cuts.

Parameters
ensThe ensemble.
requests[n_requests] ordered pairs with margins (absolute lengths). A request whose B body this rank does not hold is ignored. The lists need NOT agree across ranks.
n_requestsNumber of requests; 0 still enters every collective.
haloFilled in. Patches of the requested B bodies are replaced; pair is left untouched.
Returns
MFTL_SUCCESS, or MFTL_ERROR on invalid arguments.
Collective:
Must be called by every rank of the ensemble's communicator. Over comm_graph; the collective sequence is fixed by the neighbour count, not by the requests.

int MFTLExtendSpatialHaloNearBodies ( MFTL_Ensemble * ens,
const double * tolerance,
MFTL_SpatialHalo * halo )

The same exchange with the candidate pairs DERIVED from the published extents instead of enumerated – the form contact detection uses.

An all-pairs request list is \(O(B^2)\) per rank (134 million records at 16 384 bodies). Instead the arriving records are binned into a uniform grid whose cell is at least 2 x widest half-width + largest envelope, so overlapping extents lie in adjacent cells and each query visits 27 cells: \(O(\text{local bodies} + \text{records})\). Body B travels towards body A only when A < B (plus A == B when self_pairs), with envelope MAX(tol_A, tol_B).

On return halo->pair holds, ascending and unique, every (A, B) such that this rank holds A and can see B and their extents are within the envelope.

haloFilled in; EVERY patch is replaced and pair rebuilt.
Returns

Collective:

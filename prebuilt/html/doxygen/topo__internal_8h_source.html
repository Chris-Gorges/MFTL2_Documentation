1/**
2 * @file topo_internal.h
3 * @author Christian Gorges
4 * @date 2026-09-25
5 * @ingroup mftl_topology_change
6 *
7 * @brief Internal interfaces of the topology-change module.
8 *
9 * ## THE FILES AND WHAT EACH MAY DO
10 *
11 * topo_comm.c EVERY MPI call of the module, and nothing else. The
12 * same discipline as src/remeshing/remesh_comm.c: the
13 * algorithm files are pure local computation over data a
14 * collective handed them, which is what keeps the
15 * deadlock argument checkable by reading one file.
16 * topo_detect.c the pair predicate and the event list's canonical form
17 * topo_cluster.c lattice, clusters (union-find), extents, windows
18 * topo_reconstruct.c rasterisation, the bias, marching cubes, local meshes
19 * topo_split_bodies.c component labelling, ids, object construction
20 * topo_driver.c the two public entry points, in collective order
21 *
22 * ## THE COLLECTIVE ORDER
23 *
24 * Every rank executes the same sequence of collectives in the same order,
25 * whatever it holds. Nothing here returns early on a LOCAL count: a rank with
26 * no triangles of a cluster still enters every exchange with empty buffers.
27 */

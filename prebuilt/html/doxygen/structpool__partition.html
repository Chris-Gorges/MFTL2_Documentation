Detailed Description

The three runs a compacted pool falls into, and the map term they imply.

n_kept_owned survivors from the ownership prefix, then n_ghost surviving ghosts, then n_created entities this sweep appended. Swapping the last two restores the prefix, and partition_remap is that swap expressed on indices.

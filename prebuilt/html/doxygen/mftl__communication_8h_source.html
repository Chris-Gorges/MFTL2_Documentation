1/**
2 * @file mftl_communication.h
3 * @author Christian Gorges
4 * @date 2026-08-14
5 * @ingroup mftl_communication
6 *
7 * @brief Distributed memory: redistribution, ghost synchronisation, the spatial
8 * halo, and the exchange engine they share.
9 *
10 * Three kinds of neighbour data:
11 *
12 * | Call | Question | Result |
13 * |---|---|---|
14 * | MFTLRedistribute() | the mesh moved -- who owns what now? | re-partitioned pools with a vertex-complete topological halo |
15 * | MFTLUpdateGhostPositions() | the mesh deformed -- refresh my ghosts | ghost positions pulled from their owners; topology untouched |
16 * | MFTLExtendSpatialHaloNearBodies() | what of body B is NEAR my share of body A? | one shadow object per nearby foreign body |
17 *
18 * **Ownership and halo rule.** A vertex belongs to the rank whose Cartesian
19 * cell contains its position, a triangle to the rank whose cell contains its
20 * cached centre (MFTLGetOwnerProc()). An owned triangle is additionally sent to
21 * the owner of each of its three corners and of each of its three edge
22 * neighbours; that surplus IS the ghost layer. It is VERTEX-COMPLETE (every
23 * triangle incident to an owned vertex is present locally, which makes vertex
24 * normals communication-free) and gives every owned triangle all three edge
25 * neighbours.
26 *
27 * **Wire format.** Only topology, identity and vertex POSITIONS travel
28 * (40 B per vertex, 40 B per triangle with its three half-edges). Half-edge ids
29 * (\f$3\,t_\mathrm{gid}+k\f$), `next`/face (arithmetic), twins and
30 * `out_he_idx` are reconstructed by the receiver.
31 *
32 * **Postcondition of a redistribution.** Valid topology, valid positions,
33 * #MFTL_DERIVED_TOPOLOGY set; `tris.area/normal/center` and `verts.normal`
34 * UNSPECIFIED. With redistribution the per-timestep loop is
35 *
36 * 1. move owned vertices #MFTL_FOREACH_OWNED_VERTEX
37 * 2. refresh ghost positions MFTLUpdateGhostPositions()
38 * 3. geometry of ALL triangles, ghosts too MFTLComputeTriangleGeometry(obj, obj->tris.n)
39 * 4. re-partition MFTLRedistribute()
40 * 5. (if geometry is read afterwards) MFTLEnsembleUpdate() / MFTLEnsembleComputeTriangleGeometry()
41 *
42 * Step 2 precedes 3 because a triangle's corners may belong to three ranks; step
43 * 3 must cover ghosts because redistribution routes ghosts by their cached
44 * centre too, and two ranks disagreeing about a ghost's destination silently
45 * cost an owned triangle an edge neighbour (a Debug assertion checks the
46 * centres).
47 *
48 * **Preconditions.** MFTLRegisterMpiTypes() has been called; every rank added
49 * the same bodies in the same order (body ids are the names on the wire); the
50 * triangle centres of every local object are current, ghosts included. The
51 * generators leave them current, so a generated ensemble can be redistributed
52 * at once; after vertices move, step 3 restores them. A body whose
53 * #MFTL_DERIVED_GEOMETRY bit is clear -- no pass over every triangle since the
54 * last ghost sync, topology change or redistribution -- is recomputed before it
55 * is routed, counted in `MFTLCommStats.n_objects_centres_recomputed`, with one
56 * warning per process; this covers skipping step 3, running it over the owned
57 * triangles only, running it before step 2, and redistributing twice. Moving
58 * owned vertices after step 3 cannot be seen; every holder then routes by the
59 * same old centres, which is consistent but one step behind. Debug also checks
60 * every centre and asserts.
61 *
62 * Theory: src/communication/README.md.
63 */

268/* ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
269 * A redistribution also updates the OBJECT LIST: a rank holds an #MFTL_Object
270 * exactly for the bodies it holds entities of, so bodies that left are
271 * destroyed and bodies that arrived are created. Hence an `MFTL_Object*`, a list
272 * slot and `n_local_objects` do NOT survive a redistribution; the body id does:

274 * MFTLRedistribute(ens);
275 * obj = MFTLEnsembleFindLocalObject(ens, body_gid); // may be MFTL_NULL now
276 *
277 * A body without entities on any rank ends up held by nobody; its id stays
278 * allocated.

281/**
282 * @brief Re-partitions every local object with the geometric owner rule, in one
283 * communication round for the whole ensemble.
284 *
285 * 1. **Analysis.** Every entity's owner is recomputed (MFTLGetOwnerProc()); each
286 * OWNED triangle is destined for its owner plus the owners of its corners and
287 * edge neighbours. Entities this rank keeps are marked, not packed. Ghost
288 * triangles are never forwarded (their owner sends the authoritative copy).
289 * Bodies nobody saw change are left alone (MFTLRedistributeSetSkipUnchangedBodies()).
290 * 2. **Route.** One `MPI_Allreduce` (LOR) decides whether any destination lies
291 * outside the 26-neighbourhood; if so the exchange uses `comm_cart`.
292 * 3. **Exchange.** Vertices, triangles and one 7-int header per (body,
293 * destination) as three payloads of one MFTLExchangeStart(); all bodies share
294 * the messages, so the message count is independent of the body count.
295 * 4. **Rebuild.** Kept entities are compacted in place into the layout
296 * `[kept owned | new owned | kept ghost | new ghost]`; arrivals are
297 * de-duplicated by global id; only half-edges that lost their twin are
298 * re-paired; `out_he_idx` and the derived arrays are emitted inline. Bodies
299 * that arrived are created, bodies left empty destroyed.
300 *
301 * @param ens The ensemble; every local object is repartitioned in place. Its
302 * triangle centres must be current, ghosts included
303 * (MFTLEnsembleComputeTriangleGeometry(); a body whose
304 * #MFTL_DERIVED_GEOMETRY bit is clear is recomputed first, see the
305 * file comment; asserted in Debug).
306 * @return #MFTL_SUCCESS, or #MFTL_ERROR on a NULL ensemble.
307 *
308 * @collective Ranks holding no bodies must call it too; an ensemble without
309 * bodies is a no-op.
310 * @warning Invalidates every pool index, every #MFTL_Object pointer and all
311 * cached geometry except vertex positions (see the file comment).
312 */
313int MFTLRedistribute(MFTL_Ensemble *ens);

316 * @struct MFTL_OwnerMap
317 * @brief Caller-supplied destination rank for every entity of one body.
318 *
319 * Replaces the geometric rule for one redistribution. The distributed remesher
320 * uses it twice: the SPANNING strategy shifts the cut planes, the DISPERSED
321 * strategy sends each body to the owner of its centroid. Everything downstream
322 * (halo surplus, de-duplication, twin repair) is indifferent to where the

337} MFTL_OwnerMap;

473/* ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
474 * 6. Spatial halo
475 *
476 * The decomposition is geometric, so two bodies in contact are cut by the same
477 * rank boundaries, and the rank holding one side of a contact may hold nothing
478 * of the other body. No topological halo growth can help (the bodies share no
479 * edges), so the triangles of body B within a margin of this rank's share of
480 * body A are shipped here explicitly.
481 *
482 * They are delivered as a SHADOW OBJECT per body -- a self-consistent
483 * #MFTL_Object with `n_local == 0`, twins linked within the patch, derived
484 * arrays built -- and never appended to the body's own pools. Appending would
485 * silently change every pass bounded by `tris.n` (the Morton box,
486 * MFTLMeanEdgeLength(), the vertex-normal scatter, MFTLRebuildTwins(), which
487 * would weld bodies sharing vertex slots), and the arriving vertex ids would
488 * break either vertex completeness or local id uniqueness. The patches are not
489 * in `ens->local_objects`, so redistribution and ghost syncs never see them.
490 *
491 * The halo is TRANSIENT: redistribution keeps only owned triangles, so it cannot
492 * survive a repartition. Each extension REPLACES earlier patches. Order:
493 * redistribute -> MFTLSpatialHaloResolveRadii() -> MFTLExtendSpatialHaloNearBodies()
494 * -> contact detection.
495 * ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ */
496
497/**
498 * @struct MFTL_SpatialHaloPair
499 * @brief One candidate body pair kept by the broad phase.
500 *
501 * `body_a < body_b`: the smaller id is the A side, whose OWNED triangles report
502 * contacts; only the B side travels. That asymmetry is the "reported once"
503 * rule. A SELF pair (`body_a == body_b`) appears only when
504 * #MFTL_SpatialHalo::self_pairs is set; the traversal then restores "reported
505 * once" by triangle id. A pair is present on this rank iff it holds body A and
506 * can see body B (locally, as a patch, or both).
507 */
508typedef struct MFTL_SpatialHaloPair
509{
510 int body_a; ///< Smaller body id. Held by this rank; its owned triangles report.
511 int body_b; ///< Larger body id -- or the SAME id for a self pair. Held here, patched here, or both.
512} MFTL_SpatialHaloPair;

514/**
515 * @struct MFTL_SpatialHalo
516 * @brief A sparse, body-id-keyed directory of shadow objects and body radii.
517 *
518 * `body_gid` is strictly ascending; entry `e` describes body `body_gid[e]`. Use
519 * MFTLSpatialHaloPatch() / MFTLSpatialHaloRadius(), which binary-search. The
520 * directory holds the bodies this rank HOLDS plus the bodies it RECEIVED A PATCH
521 * FOR (the latter are exactly those it holds nothing of); other entries are

615 * otherwise measure as domain-wide. Twelve MIN slots per body go through
616 * MFTLEnsembleReduceBodies(). The result is then published one hop along
617 * `comm_graph`, because a rank deciding what to SEND needs the size of the body
618 * it sends TOWARDS, which it may hold nothing of.

628/**
629 * @brief Exchanges the explicitly requested patches and rebuilds the affected
630 * shadow objects.
631 *
632 * 1. Each rank publishes one record per body it HOLDS -- `(body id, centre,
633 * half-width, global radius, envelope)`, nine doubles -- to its graph
634 * neighbours (count allgather + non-blocking allgatherv, overlapped with
635 * computing per-triangle extents). Payload `O(local bodies x degree)`.
636 * 2. For each request whose B body it holds, a rank sends every owned triangle
637 * of B (and its vertices) whose extent, widened by the margin, is near a
638 * neighbour's published extent of A. Per destination each triangle is packed
639 * once, however many partners want it.
640 * 3. One MFTLExchangeStart() with two payloads; one patch per arriving body is
641 * rebuilt with MFTLBuildTopologyFromIndexList(), and the transmitted boundary
642 * flags overwrite the derived ones (a patch's cut rim is not a mesh boundary).
643 *
644 * **Extents are a centre plus a half-width**, compared by minimum-image
645 * distance between centres; a (lo, hi) box would miss contacts across a
646 * periodic seam and, for any share containing one seam-straddling triangle,
647 * span the whole domain and ship everything (measured at np = 27: 314 triangles
648 * with boxes against 56). A rank's own extent is measured in the raw and the
649 * half-domain-shifted frame and the tighter kept. The per-triangle test is
650 * widened by the margin and so is the body-level pre-filter, which keeps the
651 * pre-filter strictly more permissive than the test it short-cuts.
652 *
653 * @param ens The ensemble.
654 * @param requests `[n_requests]` ordered pairs with margins (absolute lengths).
655 * A request whose B body this rank does not hold is ignored.
656 * The lists need NOT agree across ranks.
657 * @param n_requests Number of requests; 0 still enters every collective.
658 * @param halo Filled in. Patches of the requested B bodies are replaced;
659 * `pair` is left untouched.
660 * @return #MFTL_SUCCESS, or #MFTL_ERROR on invalid arguments.
661 *
662 * @collective Over `comm_graph`; the collective sequence is fixed by the
663 * neighbour count, not by the requests.
664 */
665int MFTLExtendSpatialHalo(MFTL_Ensemble *ens, const MFTL_SpatialHaloRequest *requests, int n_requests, MFTL_SpatialHalo *halo);

667/**

669 * extents instead of enumerated -- the form contact detection uses.
670 *
671 * An all-pairs request list is \f$O(B^2)\f$ per rank (134 million records at
672 * 16 384 bodies). Instead the arriving records are binned into a uniform grid
673 * whose cell is at least `2 x widest half-width + largest envelope`, so
674 * overlapping extents lie in adjacent cells and each query visits 27 cells:
675 * \f$O(\text{local bodies} + \text{records})\f$. Body B travels towards body A
676 * only when A < B (plus A == B when `self_pairs`), with envelope
677 * `MAX(tol_A, tol_B)`.
678 *
679 * On return `halo->pair` holds, ascending and unique, every `(A, B)` such that
680 * this rank holds A and can see B and their extents are within the envelope.

684 * #MFTL_NULL for zero (overlap only).

686 * @return #MFTL_SUCCESS, or #MFTL_ERROR on a NULL ensemble or halo.

688 * @collective Over `comm_graph`.

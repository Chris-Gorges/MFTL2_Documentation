Detailed Description

Detection: which pairs of triangles are a thin feature, and of which kind.

Author
Christian Gorges
Date
2026-09-25

THE PREDICATE

Two triangles A and B – of one body or of two – are an EVENT when

  1. they share no vertex (MFTLEnsembleIntersectObjects' self filter);
  2. their unit normals OPPOSE: n_A . n_B <= -cos(theta);
  3. each lies on the same side of the other, as seen along its normal: s_A = n_A . (c_B - c_A) and s_B = n_B . (c_A - c_B) both negative means each is BEHIND the other – the gap between them is inside the body, a thread or sheet: BREAKUP. Both positive means each is in FRONT of the other – the gap is outside, a film: COALESCENCE. Mixed signs are a fold seen edge-on, not a thin feature, and are not events;
  4. their distance is within the threshold of THAT kind.

Condition 3 is what the original design's detection criteria left implicit, and it is the one that makes the same machinery serve both directions of a topology change: the RT cap rolling onto its own stem and a jet's neck thinning are both "two opposing triangles within delta", and only the side the gap is on tells them apart.

THE CANONICAL LIST

Detection is local and many pairs describe one feature – a neck is found once per triangle pair around it, and a pair across a rank boundary by both ranks' owners in general. Every event point is therefore snapped to a cell of a quarter of max(smaller active threshold, dx) – the lattice spacing too, because a bias bump is at least 1.5 dx wide and events closer than that are one feature (a quantum on the threshold alone shredded one jet neck into 779 events) – and events are merged on (bodies, kind, cell), keeping the smallest gap. After the gather, the same canonicalisation over the whole list makes it a pure function of the geometry: identical on every rank and at every process count.

The cheap half of the predicate, run before the exact narrow phase: only OPPOSING normals can be an event.

On a smooth surface nearly every triangle within the envelope of another has a nearly parallel normal, and the exact proximity test costs two orders of magnitude more than this dot product. See MFTL_BVHBuildParams::pair_filter.

Detailed Description

De-duplicates each REMOTE destination's vertex list on construction.

A vertex is a candidate once per incident triangle bound for a destination. One bit per (vertex, destination slot) makes the test-and-set O(1) instead of a later sort + unique. Slots are handed out on first contact; 32 suffice on the neighbour route (at most 26 remote destinations). On the global route a destination beyond 32 gets MFTL_DEDUP_NO_BIT and its list is sorted instead; correctness never depends on the bound.

Detailed Description

Topology changes by distributed signed-distance rasterisation and marching cubes, on the partition as it stands.

One predicate, both directions

Two triangles whose unit normals oppose, \(\mathbf n_A\cdot\mathbf n_B \le -\cos\theta\), and that are closer than a threshold are an EVENT. Which threshold, and what happens, is decided by the side the gap is on: each triangle BEHIND the other means the gap is inside the liquid – a thread or a sheet, BREAKUP at \(\delta_b\); each in FRONT means a film of the other phase – COALESCENCE at \(\delta_c\). Within one body or between two.

Reconstruction

The bodies an event touches are grouped into clusters, and each cluster is rasterised as the signed distance to the UNION of its surfaces, signed with angle-weighted pseudo-normals, on a global lattice whose node planes lie on every domain wall and every rank cut plane. A smooth bump of \(\pm\tfrac{\beta}{2} \delta\) at every event decides the change with margin; marching cubes then triangulates the zero level set.

Nothing migrates

Each rank rasterises the triangles it OWNS into a window and neighbours merge the overlap by a commutative, associative rule (MFTLSdfMerge()), so the field is bit-identical to a serial rasterisation. Each rank extracts its own cells plus one ghost layer; vertices are named by lattice edge, so the pieces fit with no stitching. Components are labelled by the smallest triangle key, ids made dense with MFTLEnsembleExscanBodies(), and the result redistributed to the canonical partition.

Note
All of the module's MPI is in one file, src/topology_change/topo_comm.c.

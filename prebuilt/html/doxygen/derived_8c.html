Detailed Description

The derived connectivity arrays, and the Morton renumbering of the pools.

Author
Christian Gorges
Date
2026-08-14

Only two derived arrays exist, tris.verts and tris.neighbors. Everything else a "flattened" half-edge mesh is often given was removed after counting consumers: a second (sometimes unwrapped) copy of the positions and a stored SFC permutation (both replaced by one index space, see MFTLObjectReorderMorton()), per-half-edge vertex pairs (edges are formed in registers), and a vertex-to-triangle CSR (measured as a dead heat against the triangle scatter for vertex normals, docs/analysis.md §1.5).

◆ build_topology_arrays()

void build_topology_arrays ( MFTL_Object * obj)
static

Emits tris.verts and tris.neighbors in one streaming pass.

Both are functions of the half-edge records at the same index: 

verts[3t+k]     == he[3t+(k+2)%3].vertex_idx     origin of half-edge 3t+k
neighbors[3t+k] == he[3t+k].twin_idx / 3         triangle across it

They are stored for TRAFFIC: a connectivity-only loop streams 12 B per triangle from one of them instead of the 48 B of three half-edge records.

rebuild_object() in src/communication/redistribute.c emits the same two arrays inside its relink loop; inv_derived_matches_topology() (tests/test_mesh_invariants.h) pins both spellings to this convention.

Parameters
objThe mesh. tris.verts and tris.neighbors are rewritten.

void MFTLObjectBuildDerived ( MFTL_Object * obj)

Rebuilds tris.verts and tris.neighbors from the half-edge records in one streaming pass and sets MFTL_DERIVED_TOPOLOGY (MFTL_DERIVED_GEOMETRY is left as it was).

verts[3t+k] is the ORIGIN of half-edge 3t+k and neighbors[3t+k] the triangle across it (MFTL_ERROR without a twin). Call after any topology change that did not rebuild them itself.

Orders the triangles spatially and remaps every reference.

The key is the SMALLEST of the triangle's three (already Morton-ordered) vertex indices: spatial by construction, three loads and two compares, no centroid (and no question of what a centroid means across a periodic seam), and independent of which corner is called corner 0.

◆ MFTLObjectReorderMorton()

void MFTLObjectReorderMorton ( MFTL_Object * obj)

Renumbers both pools along a Morton curve, so that neighbours in space are neighbours in memory.

The permutation is APPLIED to the pools rather than stored, so there remains one index space and one copy of every coordinate:

  1. a Morton key per vertex from verts.pos, quantised to 21 bits per axis against the object's own bounding box with one common scale (cubic cells);
  2. a stable sort of [0, n_local) and, separately, of [n_local, n), so the ownership prefix survives; all vertex arrays are permuted and he.vertex_idx remapped;
  3. triangles are ordered by their smallest (already Morton-ordered) vertex index, again owned and ghost separately; records, half-edge triples and geometry arrays move together, twin_idx and out_he_idx are remapped;
  4. the derived arrays are rebuilt.

Global ids are untouched; pool indices are not stable across the call. A body straddling a periodic seam gets a domain-wide box and coarser cells – a locality cost only. No time benefit has been measured on the development machine (docs/analysis.md); its value is the single index space and LBVH locality.

Parameters
objThe mesh; both pools are permuted in place.
Rank-local:
Performs no MPI. Each rank operates on its own partition. Each rank orders its own partition; no MPI.

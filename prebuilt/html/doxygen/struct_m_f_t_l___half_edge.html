Detailed Description

One directed edge of one triangle – the entire mesh topology.

Each undirected edge of a closed mesh is two opposite half-edges; on an open surface the boundary edges have no partner and carry twin_idx == MFTL_ERROR.

Only two topological fields are stored: vertex_idx (the vertex the half-edge ENDS at) and twin_idx. next, prev and the triangle are arithmetic on the index, and \(\mathrm{origin}(h) = \mathrm{target}(\mathrm{prev}(h))\) (mftl_topology.h). 16 bytes, so a triangle's three half-edges span one or two cache lines.
